GenderLens is a fun, private AI camera. Point it at a face and it shows, in real time, whether the face looks male or female, and how sure the model is.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your phone. No images, results or identifiers are saved or uploaded.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet access.

THOUGHTFUL AI
• Trained on FairFace, a public dataset balanced across ethnicities, ages and genders, and tested for consistent accuracy across groups.
• Shows "Not sure" instead of guessing when confidence is low.
• Smooths results over several frames, so the label doesn't flicker.
• Detects several faces at once, with front and back cameras.

PLEASE NOTE
GenderLens estimates appearance only. It can be wrong, and it says nothing about anyone's gender identity. It's for entertainment: only scan people who are happy to be scanned, and never use it to judge or make decisions about anyone.
