# GenderLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE] · Version 1

GenderLens ("the app") is provided by [DEVELOPER_LEGAL_NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: **the app processes camera images only on your device, and we collect nothing.**

## 1. What the app processes

- **Live camera images.** When the scanner is open, camera frames are analysed in your device's memory to find faces and estimate whether each face looks male or female. Each frame is discarded right after it is analysed.
- **Face data.** The face position and the cropped face image exist in memory for a fraction of a second. No face template, face geometry or identifier is created for recognising anyone, and nothing is kept after the frame is processed.
- **Results.** Estimates are shown on screen only. They are not saved, logged or shared.

## 2. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no access to the internet. We cannot see your images or results, and we have no way to receive them.

## 3. What is stored on your device

One setting: whether you accepted this policy, and which version. It is stored in the app's private storage and deleted when you withdraw consent in Settings or uninstall the app.

## 4. Permissions

- **Camera**: required to scan faces. You can revoke it at any time in system settings; the app will stop scanning.

The app does not request access to your photos, microphone, location, contacts or any other data.

## 5. Third-party components

The app includes Google ML Kit Face Detection and TensorFlow Lite, which run entirely on the device. The Android build removes the internet permission, so these components cannot transmit data. On iOS the app makes no network requests.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not scan children without their parent's or guardian's consent.

## 7. Scanning other people

Only scan people who have agreed to it. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply where you use the app.

## 8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can delete everything the app stores by withdrawing consent in Settings or uninstalling it. Residents of the EU/EEA, UK, California, China and other regions keep all rights granted by local law, and can contact us at the address below.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will ask for your consent again before scanning.
